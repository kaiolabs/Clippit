# Code of Conduct

Clippit follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, criticize ideas and not people, and assume good intent.
Harassment, insults and discrimination are not accepted. Report problems privately to the maintainers
through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/kaiolabs/Clippit/security/advisories/new) or
by contacting the repository owner; reports are handled confidentially.
